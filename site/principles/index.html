The thinking behind the work

Built to stay yours.

GoreeCloud is a personal hobby and learning project. The priority is software that feels worthwhile to build, useful to own, and maintainable over time—not growth targets or a commercial platform promise.

01 / PRINCIPLE

Ownership over dependence

Build around data and tools that can be understood, moved, backed up and maintained without relying on a single provider.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Privacy without theatrics

Minimize collection, preserve consent and separate privacy choices from technical security claims.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Security with evidence

Treat threats seriously and describe protection only when controls are implemented, active and verified.

04 / PRINCIPLE

Clarity over complexity

Make the information architecture navigable and the visual hierarchy deliberate, responsive and inclusive.

05 / PRINCIPLE

Open where appropriate

Prefer open-source foundations, transparent engineering and proper upstream attribution without exposing private personal records.

06 / PRINCIPLE

Progress over promises

A functioning preview, successful test, merged source, accepted release and production deployment are different achievements.
