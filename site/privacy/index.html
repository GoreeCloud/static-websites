GoreeShield · Privacy

Your information. Your choices.

GoreeShield governs privacy preferences, consent, transparency, tracking prevention and retention. It is distinct from Wardveil cybersecurity and from the data access responsibilities of each application.

Privacy requirement

Consent and control

Purpose-bound data use, clear choices and the ability to review or revoke supported permissions.
