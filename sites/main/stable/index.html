Stable means exact evidence

No inheritance, no guesswork, no marketing shortcuts.

A Stable extension does not make its parent platform Stable. A Stable design system does not make every consumer Stable. Historical Stable releases that have been superseded, reopened for qualification, or retired are not presented here as current Stable products.

What is intentionally excluded

Development and Release Candidate work stays out of the Stable catalog.

Active stabilization

Launcher, Keyboard, Gallery, Index, Search, Gateway, Monitor, Backup, and other active stabilization work remain outside this page until their own Stable gates are satisfied.

Historical Stable baselines

Older accepted releases are not treated as current when qualification has been reopened or the product line has moved to a new architecture.

Retired surfaces

The former multi-site public website portfolio is retired and is not listed as a current Stable product merely because historical acceptance evidence remains preserved.
