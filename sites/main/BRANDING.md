# GoreeCloud Website Branding

Branding authority: `GoreeCloud/branding-assets`.

The website must resolve GoreeCloud-controlled brand assets from the canonical branding repository and its machine-readable catalogs. Website-local public assets are synchronized publication derivatives only; they do not become independent branding authorities.

## Current canonical source

The current website identity sources are synchronized from canonical branding main revision `c0fe79ed59e15087166a543c47eac67ef382f3fd`. That revision includes the approved 2026 visual-identity revamps for Privacy Shield, GoreeCloud AI, and GoreeCloud Network, plus the owner-approved Everkeep Continuity Core identity in addition to the earlier placeholder-remediation work.

The parent GoreeCloud mark used at `assets/brand/goreecloud-logo.svg` derives from `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`.

Product artwork under `assets/products/` derives from the matching `products/<product>/app-icon.svg` source. Platform-system artwork under `assets/systems/` derives from the matching canonical system source. Firefox-specific GoreeCloud artwork under `assets/firefox/` either derives from the canonical product source or from an approved Firefox-extension identity source.

The synchronized product and system derivatives represented by the canonical branding catalog have been checked by Git blob identity against the corresponding `GoreeCloud/branding-assets` source. A consumer copy must be replaced whenever its canonical asset changes.

## Placeholder prohibition

Required GoreeCloud identity must never be represented here by a single letter, initials, a generic dot, a blank shell, a fabricated symbol, or another improvised placeholder.

When approved artwork is unavailable, the dependent presentation must remain incomplete until the canonical identity is created in `GoreeCloud/branding-assets`; the website must not manufacture its own replacement.

## Third-party artwork

Third-party artwork, when used in a reviewed historical or compatibility context, remains governed by its own source authority and licensing or trademark constraints rather than the GoreeCloud branding repository.

## Truth boundary

Branding does not establish implementation, release, privacy, security, continuity, conformance, health, integration, or production-readiness claims. Those states require separate authoritative evidence.
