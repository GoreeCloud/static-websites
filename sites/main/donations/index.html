Support software that stays in your hands.

GoreeCloud is built around independent, privacy-respecting, user-first software. Whether you contribute skills, feedback, visibility, or eventually a voluntary donation, your support helps move that vision forward.

What support makes possible

Better software, without trading away control.

Support can sustain the work of designing, building, documenting, reviewing, and maintaining software that puts user choice first. These are priorities, not promises of specific spending or completed features.

Independent development

Advance first-party applications and shared systems while keeping ownership, portability, and replaceable dependencies central.

Privacy and security

Keep respectful defaults, careful reviews, and responsible handling of data central to the engineering process—not a trade for funding.

Long-term maintainability

Support attention to accessibility, documentation, testing, resilience, and lasting usefulness as technology evolves.

Common questions

Supporting GoreeCloud, clearly explained.

Is support required?

No. Support is optional. The ad-free, user-first direction does not depend on whether someone contributes.

Can I donate money today?

Not through this site. No financial checkout is active or endorsed here. We will identify a verified destination before asking for funds.

Do donors get exclusive benefits?

No donor-only features or benefits are promised. Future contribution terms, if any, will be published clearly before payment is offered.
