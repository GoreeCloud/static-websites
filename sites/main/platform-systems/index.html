Integral Platform Systems

Nine shared systems. Nine distinct authorities.

GoreeCloud applications remain specialized products, while these cross-cutting systems provide shared management, privacy, security, continuity, experience, coordination, identity, policy, and observability foundations. Integration must be real—not a badge or marketing label.

Boundary: GoreeCloud Sync is separately governed synchronization capability. It is not a tenth Integral Platform System.
MG

Administration, management, and control

GoreeCloud Manager

The central administration authority and management plane for applications, services, infrastructure, devices, configuration, lifecycle controls, dashboards, and administrative action.

PS

Privacy protection and data control

Privacy Shield

The platform-wide privacy authority for privacy-by-default controls, consent, permitted use, minimization, retention, sharing, tracking protection, and privacy evidence.

WV

Security, defense, and platform protection

Wardveil Security

The security authority for threat protection, hardening, vulnerability management, credentials, defensive controls, security posture, risk, and security evidence.

EK

Resilience, preservation, recovery, and continuity

Everkeep

The continuity and preservation system for recoverability, portability, migration readiness, restoration verification, succession, and long-term information survival.

GU

Design, user experience, and visual consistency

Glaze UI

The shared visual and interaction language governing design tokens, materials, typography, navigation, accessibility, responsive behavior, state presentation, and cross-platform consistency.

ME

Ecosystem coordination, discovery, and integration

GoreeCloud Mesh

The coordination fabric for service and capability discovery, relationships, approved contracts, APIs, events, interoperability, dependency context, and evidence routing.

ID

Identity, authentication, authorization, and trust

GoreeCloud Identity

The identity authority for accounts, authentication, single sign-on, sessions, device and service identity, federation, claims, trust relationships, and authorization context.

PL

Policy definition, evaluation, decision, and distribution

GoreeCloud Policy

The shared policy framework for policy schemas, evaluation, decisions, precedence, distribution, enforcement coordination, explanation, provenance, freshness, and policy evidence.

OB

Operational health, telemetry, diagnostics, and evidence

GoreeCloud Observability

The operational-health authority for metrics, logs, events, traces, performance, availability, dependency health, diagnostics, correlation, provenance, freshness, and operational evidence.
