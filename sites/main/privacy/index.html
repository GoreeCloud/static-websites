GoreeCloud Privacy Shield

Privacy authority that travels with the operation.

Privacy Shield is GoreeCloud's platform-wide privacy, consent, data-governance, minimization, transparency, and user-control authority. It defines shared privacy decisions while runtime ownership stays with the application or service that actually performs the work.

Authorization model

Identity answers who. Privacy also asks why, where, and for how long.

Privacy Shield evaluates an operation against declared data use, policy, consent, purpose, processing zone, destination, retention, and other applicable constraints before an authorized runtime proceeds.

Application manifestPolicyConsentPurposeDestinationRetention
The source-level Privacy Shield 2.0 decision path supports ALLOW, DENY, ALLOW WITH CONSTRAINTS, and REQUIRE USER DECISION outcomes. Constraints intersect authority rather than broadening it.

Privacy capabilities

One authority, distributed enforcement.

Data-use authorization

Shared contracts cover purpose limitation, consent, permitted processing zones and destinations, retention constraints, disclosure rules, and privacy evidence.

Browser privacy runtime

GoreeCloud Browser remains a privileged Privacy Shield runtime for browser-specific ad and tracker blocking, tracking-parameter cleanup, reviewed local-resource substitution, exceptions, and privacy controls.

Minimized status & receipts

Privacy status should prefer minimal derived state and evidence instead of collecting raw browsing, DNS, network, message, file, clipboard, location, or credential payloads merely for presentation.

Runtime ownership

Privacy Shield does not become a universal proxy.

Applications and services keep authority over the actions they actually perform while adopting the applicable Privacy Shield contracts and adapters.

GoreeCloud DNS

Owns DNS privacy filtering and DNS-policy execution when its Privacy Shield adapter is implemented and accepted for that scope.

GoreeCloud Network

Owns privacy-relevant encrypted networking when its network-privacy adapter is implemented and accepted.

Wardveil Security

Owns security and protection. It may provide security evidence to Privacy Shield and present privacy status without replacing Privacy Shield authority.

Everkeep

Coordinates deletion and lifecycle effects across backups and recovery material while retaining continuity and recovery authority.

Glaze UI

Defines privacy-facing interaction and state presentation while the underlying privacy decision remains provider-owned and evidence-bound.

Current acceptance boundary

Shared foundations do not make every adapter production-ready.

The Browser privacy core and platform authorization foundations are source-integrated, but runtime integrations keep independent implementation and production-acceptance boundaries. Branding approval, contract validation, successful CI, or a draft adapter declaration cannot grant a Privacy Shield capability that the runtime has not implemented and validated.
