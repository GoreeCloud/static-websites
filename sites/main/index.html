Glaze UI V1.6

Depth, atmosphere, identity, and clarity—used with restraint.

Glaze is not “glass everywhere.” GoreeCloud interfaces combine solid reading surfaces, translucent navigation and contextual layers, expressive but controlled color, official iconography, deliberate asymmetry, quiet zones, responsive depth, and accessible fallbacks.

Operating principles

Control is more useful when the boundaries are visible.

P

Private by default

Minimize exposure, keep data use explicit, and avoid turning tracking or telemetry into a prerequisite for a polished experience.

R

Recoverable by design

Portability, backup, restore testing, migration, preservation, and continuity are treated as design constraints rather than afterthoughts.

N

Native-first ownership

GoreeCloud-controlled software moves toward first-party implementations while retaining narrowly justified foundational dependencies where they reduce risk or preserve compatibility.
