Operating principles

Clear boundaries make the ecosystem easier to trust.

Private by default

Minimize exposure, keep data use explicit, and avoid treating tracking or telemetry as a prerequisite for a polished experience.

Recoverable by design

Portability, backup, restore testing, migration, preservation, and continuity are design constraints rather than afterthoughts.

Native-first ownership

GoreeCloud-controlled applications are moving toward first-party implementations while retaining narrowly justified foundational dependencies where they reduce risk or preserve compatibility.
